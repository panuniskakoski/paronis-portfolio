Overview

We at Unibyte Studios worked on Spirit Trials from fall 2022 to spring 2023. I handled all graphics for the project—including Play Store art, icons, and most UI imagery—plus composed, recorded, and mixed the soundtrack, and voiced/produced character and boss dialogue.

Key contributions

  • 2D art direction, all in‑game graphics and marketing art
  • UI imagery & icon sets; collaboration on layouts
  • Original soundtrack, SFX polish, and voice production

Outcomes

  • Playable release on Google Play
  • Trailer and multiple update videos shipped
